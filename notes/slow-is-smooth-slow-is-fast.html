Slow is smooth, slow is fast

2026-09-06

Learning German has me repeating a line that is not German at all: Slow is smooth. Smooth is fast. Slow is fast.

I used to treat “slow” like failure. Now it feels like aim. Rushing only hides the mistake; going steady lets me see it — then fix it.
