In-browser object detection

Updated 2026-09-24 HKT · Research notes for the personal-site lab (not legal advice).

Recommendation

Use TensorFlow.js COCO-SSD (Apache-2.0) for the public personal-site demo.

  • Runs fully in the visitor’s browser (WebGL/CPU); no server GPU.
  • Apache-2.0 is permissive: fine for a personal site without AGPL copyleft on the whole project.
  • Mature CDN distribution (@tensorflow/tfjs + @tensorflow-models/coco-ssd).
  • Good enough for a teaching sample (COCO classes); not a claim of “Ultralytics YOLO production accuracy.”

Name the page honestly: “object detection sample (COCO-SSD)” while the podcast can still explain the YOLO idea. Do not imply the lab page loads Ultralytics weights if it does not.
